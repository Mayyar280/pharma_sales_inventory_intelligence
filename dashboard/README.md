# Dashboard

Place the final Power BI (`.pbix`) or Excel dashboard (`.xlsx`) here, together with a small number of screenshots for the GitHub README.

Suggested dashboard pages:
1. Executive Overview – total sales, records, countries, annual trend.
2. Product Performance – product class and top-product trends.
3. Customer & Distributor – customer segments and distributor concentration.
4. Channels & Sales Teams – channel and representative performance.
5. Data Quality – negative sales, mismatches, exact duplicate groups, and outlier flags.

Do not label sales quantity as inventory stock or sales value as profit.
